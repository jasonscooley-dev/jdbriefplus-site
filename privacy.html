Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026.

Publisher: Jason Cooley, the individual developer of JDBrief+. Contact: jdbriefplus@gmail.com.

Information in your workspace

JDBrief+ lets you record audio, manually enter and edit document text, organize clients and matters, and generate Word files. Recordings, document text, organizational details, preferences and generated files are stored in the app's local workspace. Microphone permission is requested when you choose to record.

A persistent device-local workspace identifier and a database key are stored using iOS SecureStore. The document database is configured to require SQLCipher. Audio and generated documents are stored in the app container. These controls do not guarantee that every exported or backed-up copy is encrypted or unrecoverable after deletion.

Cloud, AI and purchases

This device-only release does not offer sign-in accounts, automatic transcription, AI processing, remote collaboration, app-operated cloud synchronization or subscription purchases. Its configuration does not intentionally send your audio or document text to our servers or AI providers. Advertising, tracking and app analytics are not enabled by this release configuration. This describes the device-only app configuration; iOS and other service providers operate under their own settings and policies.

Sharing and other services

When you explicitly export or share a recording or Word document, your chosen destination, provider or recipient controls its copy under its own practices. JDBrief+ cannot recall those copies. iOS diagnostics, keyboard services and device backups may operate under their own settings and policies.

Retention and deletion

The default retention setting keeps local work until manual deletion. Optional retention settings remove eligible older local documents when Documents opens; matter-linked documents and unfinished recordings are protected by the app's retention rules. Trash, restore and confirmed permanent local deletion are available.

Delete this device workspace in Settings removes its local recordings, documents, organizational records and preferences. The device workspace identifier and database encryption key in iOS secure storage are not reset by this control. It does not remove other account workspaces, independent exports, backups or recipient copies. Deletion is not a forensic erasure warranty. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove your local work; keep independent exports of important files.

Your choices and responsibilities

You can manage microphone access in iOS Settings, choose whether and where to share files, and manage local retention and deletion. Record and share material only with appropriate authority and consent. JDBrief+ does not create attorney-client privilege or certify legal or regulatory compliance.

Privacy questions and future changes

For privacy questions, contact jdbriefplus@gmail.com. Do not send confidential content or credentials in a privacy inquiry. Future account, cloud, AI or subscription features require updated disclosures and any necessary consent before activation.
