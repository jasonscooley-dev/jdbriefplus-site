Support for your daily dictation.

JDBrief+ is published by Jason Cooley. For support, privacy and business inquiries, contact jdbriefplus@gmail.com.

JDBrief+ provides local recording, playback, manual document editing, client and matter organization, and Word document export. This edition requires no account or subscription. Automatic transcription, AI processing, app-operated cloud backup and collaboration are unavailable.

Recording and playback

Allow microphone access when prompted. If access was denied, open iPhone Settings, find JDBrief+, and enable its microphone permission. Start a recording, pause or resume as needed, then stop and save. Reopen saved recordings from Documents to listen again. Background recording is not enabled; check the app after locking your phone or an interruption.

Create and share a Word document

Enter or edit your text in the document review screen, save it, and generate a Word document using the built-in layout. Open the file to review it before sharing. Choose the intended destination in the iOS share sheet. Review names, dates, formatting and final content yourself; audio is not automatically converted to text in this edition.

Work without a connection

Local recording, editing and Word generation work without an app account or internet connection. A sharing destination or this support website may need internet access. Keep independent exports of important work; this edition does not provide app-operated cloud recovery.

Manage local data

Settings includes retention controls and Delete this device workspace. Export important work before confirming deletion. Local deletion does not remove copies already exported, sent to other people, or held in system backups. There is no remote JDBrief+ account to delete in this edition.

Get help

Support, privacy and business contact: jdbriefplus@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and a brief description of the issue. Do not send client information, recordings, document contents, passwords or keys.
